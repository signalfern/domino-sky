One storm in Mumbai at 7:30 AM.
Almost 6 hours later, a flight leaves Delhi 30 minutes late, under a clear sky. ✈️⛈️

Why? One plane flies many flights in a day. If its first flight is late, every flight after it is late too. Like dominoes.

For #PromptWars I built Domino Sky, a simple simulator that shows this.

Here is a short walkthrough:

1️⃣ Close an airport
Pick a city, a start time and how long it stays closed. Or pick a ready scenario like "Mumbai storm".

2️⃣ Watch the dominoes fall
A live map of India shows every plane. Green is on time, orange is a little late, red is very late. Click any flight to see why it is late, step by step, back to the storm.

3️⃣ Count the damage
See how many flights are late, how many passengers miss their connection, and which crews go over their legal working hours.

4️⃣ Fix it with AI
The app tests every recovery move: cancel a flight, use a spare plane, or make a flight wait for connecting passengers. A Gemini AI dispatcher picks the best plan and explains it in plain English. For the Mumbai storm, the best plan cuts stranded passengers from 199 to 48.

My main rule: the AI explains, the code calculates.
AI can get numbers wrong. So here the AI does no maths. A simulation engine makes every number, and the app checks each number the AI writes before you see it. If one number does not match, the answer is rejected.
